# Galaxy Arena 2

A 3D eat-and-grow arena across a whole spiral galaxy, rebuilt from scratch for WebGPU. No textures, meshes or image assets: every planet, orb, star, ring and the sky are generated by hand-written WGSL shaders at run time.

This is the second version of Galaxy Arena. Version 1 (three.js, WebGL, multiplayer) is the main game one folder up.

## Requirements

A browser with WebGPU: current Chrome or Edge (113+), Safari 26+, Firefox 141+. There is no WebGL fallback by design; browsers without WebGPU get a message pointing to Galaxy Arena 1.

## Controls

- **Mouse** or **arrow keys**: steer
- **W / S**: zoom in / out (also the mouse wheel)
- **Space**: split toward where you're heading
- **F**: spit a little mass (three spits burst a bigger ball apart)
- **Shift**: brake
- **C**: look back · **V**: lock the view direction · **I**: show the controls
- **F1** or **`**: performance overlay
- Phones: drag to steer, pinch to zoom, SPLIT / SPIT / BRAKE / BACK buttons

## What it does

- **Galaxy.** A spiral disc with a bulge, two arms, dust lanes, pink star-forming knots and a faint halo, drawn as 200,000 point stars in one draw call. Wide golden feeding grounds sit above and below the disc; orbs there are worth three times as much.
- **Food.** Up to 30,000 orbs, each with a fixed home in the galaxy. They load in even sweeps across the whole view, fade in and out, regrow 25–45 s after being eaten, and once a view has loaded new orbs only appear out of sight. Small or distant orbs become pin-point stars whose brightness, not size, carries the distance.
- **Planets.** Ten procedural worlds (cratered moon, living world, desert, banded giant with a storm, pale giant, ice giant, molten world, star, ice moon, cloud world), with rings, orbiting moons and star coronas. Every ball, moon and spit blob is one draw call.
- **Bots.** A crowd of real bots around you, six giants, three colossi a twentieth of the galaxy wide, and thousands of lightweight far-off wanderers that become real bots when they come near.
- **Power-ups.** Growth and speed ×4 / ×8 / ×16, 36 seconds each.
- **Camera.** A fly-in from the whole galaxy on every Play, a chase camera that trails your heading, first person at full zoom-in, look-back and view lock.
- **Post-processing.** Bloom (desktop), ACES tone mapping, vignette, FXAA.

## Performance

The overlay (F1) shows fps, frame time, the resolution scale and CPU time per system (input, sim, camera, food, bodies, render), plus orb, ball and star counts. If the 2-second average frame time goes over budget (21 ms desktop, 40 ms phone) the resolution drops 10% at a time, down to 60%; it climbs back when there is headroom.

- No allocation in the frame loop. Every GPU buffer is sized at start-up and written into a pre-allocated typed array.
- Orbs, stars, specks, glows, balls and rings are each a single draw call reading a storage buffer by vertex index.

## Running locally

There is no build step. Serve the folder above this one and open `galaxy-arena-2/index.html`:

```
python3 -m http.server 8767
```

`tools/harness.sh` boots the game in headless Chrome with WebGPU, saves screenshots of the home screen, intro, close, mid and far views, and reports console or shader errors.

Test hooks: `?autoplay`, `?nointro`, `?z=<zoom>`, `?mass=<mass>`, `?stats`, `?nofade`.

## Layout

```
index.html            page, HUD and styles
src/main.js           entry point and frame orchestration
src/core/             settings, input, perf, maths, GPU helpers, hashes
src/world/            galaxy shape, gameplay, food, far-off crowd, power-ups
src/render/           camera rig, star field, balls / rings / glows / specks
src/ui/               HUD
src/shaders/          all WGSL (planet, orbs, sprites, sky, rings)
src/shaders/lib/      shared WGSL (noise)
tools/harness.sh      headless capture harness
```
