How to play

Steer by touching anywhere on the screen (your thumb's resting spot is the center). Eat orbs to grow, tap SPLIT to launch half your mass at smaller players, SPIT to shoot out a bit of mass, and CHAT to talk. Grow as big as the whole map to win and reset the world.

Controls feel backwards?

Untick "Inverted controls" on the home screen.

Someone is being rude in chat

Tap the lobby pill (top-left) and tap "mute" next to their name, or untick "Player chat" on the home screen.
