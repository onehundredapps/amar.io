AMAR.IO Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

AMAR.IO is a 3D arena game. We built it to collect as little as possible.

What we collect

Nothing. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We do not sell or share any data.

What stays on your device

Your chosen player name, color and settings are saved only on your device so they are there next time.

Multiplayer and chat

When you play online, your player name, ball position, size and any chat messages you send are shared directly with other players in the same lobby (peer-to-peer) so the game works. This information is not stored on any server by us and disappears when the match ends. Chat has a profanity filter, can be turned off on the home screen, and any player can be muted from the lobby list.

Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Contact

Questions or reports: open an issue on GitHub.
